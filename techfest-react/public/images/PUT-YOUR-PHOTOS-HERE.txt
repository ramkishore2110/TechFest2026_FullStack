Copy your four gallery photos into this folder with exactly these names:

  image1.jpg
  image2.jpeg
  image3.jpg
  image4.jpg

These names are used in src/pages/Gallery.jsx.
If you use different names, edit the "photos" array in that file to match.

Tip: resize the photos to about 1200px wide first. Very large files
(several MB each) make the Gallery page slow and will lower your
Lighthouse score in Experiment 5.

You can delete this text file afterwards.
